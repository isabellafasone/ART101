Five Nights at Freddy's: Choose your Character

By Isabella Fasone

The Lore:

Five Nights at Freddy's is a survival horror video game created by Scott Cawthon and released in 2014. The game takes place at a fictional family restaurant called Freddy Fazbear's Pizza, where animatronic characters entertain children during the day. However, at night, the animatronics begin moving around the building, creating a terrifying experience for the nighttime security guard.

How the Game Works:

You play as a security guard who must survive five nights working at the restaurant. Your main job is to monitor the building using security cameras and prevent the animatronic characters from reaching your office.

  • Watch the Cameras: Keep track of where the animatronics are moving throughout the restaurant.
  • Manage your Power:Your security doors, lights, and other equipment use limited electricity. If you run out of power, you become much more vulnerable.
  • Protect your Office:Use the doors and lights to check whether animatronics are nearby and keep them from entering.
  • Survive until 6 a.m.:Each night becomes more challenging as the animatronics become more active and harder to predict.

Choose your Character:

Freddy Fazbear

The brown bear mascot of the restaurant. Freddy
is the main character of the animatronic band and
becomes an increasingly dangerous threat as the
nights progress.

Chica the Chicken

A yellow chicken animatronic who carries a
cupcake. Chica wanders through the restaurant
and can approach the office from the right side.

Bonnie the Bunny

A purple rabbit who plays the guitar. Bonnie is
one of the first animatronics players encounter as
he moves through the building toward the
security office.

Foxy the Pirate Foxy

A damaged-looking pirate fox who stays
behind a curtain in Pirate Cove. If you do not
monitor him carefully, he can suddenly
rush toward your office.

Golden Freddy

Golden Freddy is a mysterious, golden-colored
animatronic who appears unexpectedly in the security
office in Five Nights at Freddy's. He is known for his
rare appearances, frightening jumpscares, and
connection to the game's hidden story.
